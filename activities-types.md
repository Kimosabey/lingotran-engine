Here's a breakdown of all 37 activity types:
1. PC_PoS – Phrase Chain Part of Speech
Learners build or identify a chain of words based on their grammatical role (noun, verb, adjective, etc.).
2. MCQ_T2T_SP – Multiple Choice, Text to Text (Subject Pronoun)
A text prompt is shown and learners pick the correct subject pronoun from text options.
3. MCQ_T2T – Multiple Choice, Text to Text
A text-based question where learners choose the correct text answer from multiple options.
4. Inf_ConjVE_ARERIR – Infinitive & Conjugation of Verbs (AR/ER/IR)
Tests recognition and conjugation of AR, ER, IR verbs. Focuses on identifying the correct verb form.
5. Inf_ConjV_ARERIR – Infinitive & Conjugation of Verbs (AR/ER/IR)
Similar to #4 but focuses more on the conjugation exercise itself rather than recognition.
6. Type_ConjV – Typing Conjugated Verb
Learners type the correct conjugated form of a verb. No choices given — they must recall and type it.
7. Type_T2T – Typing, Text to Text
A text prompt is shown and learners type the correct text answer.
8. Vocab_Intro
Introduction to new vocabulary. Likely a teaching/display element before practice begins.
9. MCQ_P2T – Multiple Choice, Picture to Text
A picture is shown as the prompt and learners choose the correct text answer.
10. MCQ_T2P – Multiple Choice, Text to Picture
A text prompt is given and learners select the correct picture from the options.
11. MCQ_A2T – Multiple Choice, Audio to Text
An audio clip plays and learners pick the correct text answer.
12. MCQ_T2A – Multiple Choice, Text to Audio
A text prompt is shown and learners select the correct audio answer.
13. Type_A2T – Audio to Typing
Learners listen to an audio prompt and type out the correct text answer.
14. Jamboard
An interactive word-building activity where learners select words step by step from blocks representing parts of speech to build sentences.
15. Article
Practice with articles (a, an, the) in context.
16. Determiners
Exercises involving demonstrative determiners (this, that, these, those) and possessive determiners (my, your, his, etc.).
17. PD – Possessive Determiner
Focused specifically on possessive determiners (my, your, his, her, its, our, their).
18. GD – General Determiners
Broader determiner practice covering various types beyond just possessive.
19. Adjective
Practice identifying or using adjectives correctly in context.
20. Modal_Verb – Modal Verb Activity
Focused practice on modal verbs like can, should, must, would, might, etc.
21. Phrase_Rearrange – Phrase Rearrangement
Learners are given jumbled words or phrases and must put them in the correct order to form a meaningful sentence.
22. FIB_T2T – Fill in the Blank, Text to Text
A sentence with a missing word is shown and learners fill in the blank with the correct text.
23. PrepoD – Prepositional Determiner
Exercises specifically focused on prepositions used as or with determiners.
24. MCQ_ConjV – Multiple Choice, Conjugated Verb
Learners choose the correctly conjugated verb form from multiple text options.
25. MCQ_nf_ConjV – Multiple Choice, Infinitive & Conjugated Verb
Learners choose the correct infinitive or conjugated form of a verb across different tenses and persons.
26. MPInfo1_Video – Video Teaching Element
An instructional video shown to learners. It's a content delivery element, not an interactive exercise.
27. MCQ_ConjV1 – Multiple Choice, Conjugated Verb (Version 1)
Similar to #24, multiple choice for conjugated verbs — likely a variant with different verb sets or structure.
28. MCQ_Inf_ConjV1 – Multiple Choice, Infinitive & Conjugated Verb (Version 1)
Similar to #25, covering infinitive and conjugation across tenses — a variant version.
29. MPInfo1_Prompt_Review – Test Review Screen
A screen shown to learners to review what they've done, likely a summary or feedback display after a test/activity.
30. MPInfo1_Explainer – Explainer Pop-Up Before Practice
An explainer page shown before a practice exercise begins to explain what the learner is about to do.
31. UserInfo14_UserVideo – User Video Capture
Learners record a video response which is then captured and analyzed.
32. MPInfo1_Explainer1 – HTML Explainer Page
A static HTML page shown as part of the instructional sequence, similar to #30 but in a different format or position in the flow.
33. Rapid_Fire – Rapid Fire Module
A fast-paced activity where vocabulary or phrases are thrown at the learner in quick succession. Prompts come from a prompt table.
34. Subjective – Subjective Answer Activity
Learners write open-ended answers. They have formatting options like font styles available to them.
35. JBV1 – Jamboard Version 1
An earlier or specific version of the Jamboard activity (#14). Learners pick words step by step from part-of-speech blocks to build sentences.
36. Drop_In – Drop-In Activity
Quick exercises involving phrases, adverbs (time, manner, frequency), conjunctions, or numbers. Designed as short, targeted practice.
37. Speech_Recognition_Analysis – Azure Speech Analysis
Learners speak their response and Azure's speech recognition engine analyzes and evaluates what was said.
