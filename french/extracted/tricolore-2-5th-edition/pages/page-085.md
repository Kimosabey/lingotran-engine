# 5 Bon appétit!

**Stratégies**

**Translating into English (1)**

Menus often contain unfamiliar vocabulary. Try to work out the meaning of the phrases below (1–8).

- Use cognates and common sense.
- See if you know any of the words from another context.
- Think of synonyms of the English translation, e.g. plate of the day → dish of the day.
- Is there a similar expression in English?
- If you take off the circumflex and add an 's' after the vowel, it can often help you translate.
- If you're still stuck, use the glossary or a dictionary, but think about which word you're going to look up.

*(Info box, blue rounded border with a blue "i" icon:)*

The articles (words for 'the', 'a' and 'some') are often not printed on a menu, but you need to say them when ordering. They are often not said in English, but they are in French.

*Je voudrais* **du** *melon et* **une** *omelette au fromage et, comme boisson,* **de l'** *eau minérale.*
