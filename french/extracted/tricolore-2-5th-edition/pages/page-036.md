### The infinitive (l'infinitif)

When there are two verbs in a sentence, the second verb is often in the infinitive, e.g.

**On aime faire du vélo.** *We like to go cycling.*

**Nous allons rentrer demain.** *We're going to go home tomorrow.*

If you look up a verb in a dictionary or a glossary, it is usually shown in the infinitive.

If you don't know the infinitive of an irregular verb, look at the **vous** form as this is often closest to it, e.g. **vous allez** (aller), **vous avez** (avoir).

Can you work out the infinitive of these verbs?
