*(yellow box:)*

When you look up a noun in a dictionary, you will notice some letters after the word:
- 'n.m.' tells you that it is a noun and it is masculine (**le** or **un**),
- 'n.f.' tells you that it is a noun and it is feminine (**la** or **une**).

The nouns on these pages have *m* (masculine) or *f* (feminine) after them and *pl* if they are plural.
