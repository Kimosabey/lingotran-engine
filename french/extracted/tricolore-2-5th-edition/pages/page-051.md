### Dossier-langue — Grammaire 12.5

**_vouloir_ in negative sentences**

To say you don't want to do something, add **ne … pas** around the part of **vouloir**.

Je **ne** veux **pas** faire mes devoirs.
*I don't want to do my homework.*

Vous **ne** voulez **pas** aller en ville?
*Don't you want to go to town?*
