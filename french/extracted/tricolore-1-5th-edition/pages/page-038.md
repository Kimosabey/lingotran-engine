# 4C Tu as des questions?

- *ask questions*
- *practise the singular of the verb* ***avoir*** *(to have)*

**Dossier-langue** | **Grammaire 8.1, 8.2**

### Asking questions

1 Put **Est-ce que ...** in front of a statement.

**Tu as un animal.** → **Est-ce que tu as un animal?**

Look for some more examples in the interviews.

What happens when **Est-ce que ...** is followed by a vowel?

2 You can just raise your voice at the end of a sentence, e.g.

**Tu es sportif?** (rising intonation)

**Elle a un chat?** (rising intonation)

3 Use a question word, and in most cases change the order of (invert) the subject and the verb, e.g.

**Comment s'appelle-t-il/elle?**
**Comment ça s'écrit?**
**Quel âge as-tu?**
**Quel jour sommes-nous?**
**Où est le chat?**

When the subject is a pronoun (e.g. **tu**, **il**, **elle**), you need to add a hyphen when you invert. Why do you think a 't' is sometimes added?
