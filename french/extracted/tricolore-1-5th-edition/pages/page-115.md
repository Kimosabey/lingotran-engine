**Stratégies**

### Tackling a gap-fill text

You need to use both listening and reading skills to complete a gapped text based on a recording. Think about the words that might be said (in this case, items of food and drink). Work out how they will be pronounced and say the words aloud to get used to the sound.

Then think about the type of word (noun, adjective, verb, connective, etc.) that would fit in each gap. Clues such as the article (*le, la, les, du, de la, des,* etc.) may indicate whether the missing word is masculine, feminine, singular or plural.
