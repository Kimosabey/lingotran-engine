**Dossier-langue** **Grammaire 11.8**

### The future – *aller* + infinitive

You already know the verb **aller** (to go).

See how many different parts of it you can find in the messages and questions on this page.

Then look at the words which immediately follow, e.g.

**tu vas manger**

**on va organiser**

They are always the same kind of word.

What is this part of the verb called?

Why is **aller** used with another verb like this? Clue: When talking about the future, you often say what you are **going** to do.
