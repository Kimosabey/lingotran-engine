### *son, sa, ses* (his, her, its)

These words are possessive adjectives. They follow the same pattern as **mon**, **ma**, **mes** and **ton**, **ta**, **tes** (see page 22).

The word you need depends on the noun which follows, not the owner.

| masculine (un/le) | feminine (une/la) | before a vowel (un/une/l') | plural (des/les) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *son livre* | *sa maison* | *son anniversaire* | *ses parents* |
| *his book* | *his house* | *his birthday* | *his parents* |
| *her book* | *her house* | *her birthday* | *her parents* |
| *its book* | *its house* | *its birthday* | *its parents* |
