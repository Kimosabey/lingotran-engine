# Tricolore

## 5e édition

*Tricolore 5e édition* provides the engaging topics and solid grammar progression that enable your students to shine at Key Stage 3 and beyond. Combining the tried and trusted Tricolore approach with modern themes and flexible online resources, it's the only course with the heritage, rigour and flair to inspire and challenge high-ability students.

### The course features:

- systematic grammar progression, with extensive practice in the book and online
- motivating tasks set in authentic contexts, with French rubrics used throughout
- stretching stimulus materials for reading and listening, with full support for students as they become more confident linguists
- vocabulary and grammar reference sections to encourage independent learning
- support for transition, with on-the-page extension for students with greater knowledge of French from KS2
- progressive coverage of language-learning strategies
- regular focus on sound-spelling links to enable accurate pronunciation and dictation
