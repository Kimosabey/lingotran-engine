# Sommaire

**Now I can …**

- **say hello and goodbye and give my name**

| French | English |
|---|---|
| *Bonjour! Je m'appelle Lynda.* | Hello. I'm called Lynda. |
| *Comment t'appelles-tu?* | What are you called? |
| *Salut Lynda! Je m'appelle Alain.* | Hi Lynda! I'm called Alain. |
| *Au revoir!* | Bye! |

- **give my age**

| French | English |
|---|---|
| *Quel âge as-tu?* | How old are you? |
| *J'ai quatorze ans.* | I'm 14. |

- **ask people how they are and say how I am too**

| French | English |
|---|---|
| *Ça va?* | How are you? OK? |
| *Ça va bien, merci.* | Fine, thank you. |
| *Comme ci comme ça.* | So-so/Not bad. |
| *Non, pas très bien.* | No, not so good. |

- **recognise masculine and feminine nouns**

*un livre* (masculine, shown in blue) — *une table* (feminine, shown in red)

- **make nouns plural**

| Singular | | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| *un cahier* | → | *des cahiers* |
| *une trousse* | → | *des trousses* |
