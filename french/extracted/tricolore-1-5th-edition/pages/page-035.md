**Dossier-langue — Grammaire 2**

**Adjectives (1)**

Adjectives are words that describe nouns, for example, to say the **colour** and **size** of something. To say an animal is big you normally use **gros**. For people, use **grand**, which can also mean 'tall' – if you use **gros** for people it means 'fat'. The word **petit** (small) is used for both people and animals. You will learn more about adjectives later.
