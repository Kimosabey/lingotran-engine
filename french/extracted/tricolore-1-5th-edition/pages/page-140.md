## Stratégies

### Learning and revising vocabulary

It's important to keep practising a language, so you don't forget all you've learnt. Make a list of all the ways you can think of to learn and revise vocabulary. (See page 41.) Discuss with a friend which techniques work best for you.

During the holidays, choose one of these topics each week and try to practise some of the words each day.

Numbers (p19, p29 and p59)
Family and house (p29)
Animals (p41)
Colours (p41)
Clothes (p59)
Weather (p75)
Places in town (p91)
School subjects (p109)
Food and drink (p123)
Sport and music (p139)
