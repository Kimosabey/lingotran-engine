**Dossier-langue** | **Grammaire 3.1**

### Two ways of saying 'you'

Use **tu** …
- for a friend.
- for someone your own age or younger.
- for an animal.

**tu** is always singular.

Use **vous** …
- for an older person.
- for someone you don't know well.

**vous** can be singular or plural. Always use it for two or more people.
