**Stratégies**

### Using spelling patterns to improve writing

Look out for patterns that may help you to write French words correctly.

- French words ending in *-ie*

Find two school subjects which end in *-ie* in French.
What is their ending in English?

Using the same rule, how would you spell the French words for the following?

**a** photography **b** biology **c** astronomy

The next two need an acute accent, as in *géographie*:

**d** melody **e** comedy

- French words ending in *-que*

If 'music' is *la musique*, guess how to spell the French words for the following:

**f** fantastic **g** historic **h** electronic (also needs an acute accent)
