**Stratégies**

### Remembering vocabulary (2)

Here are six ways to memorise vocabulary. Try them out and see which ones work well for you.

1 Use the technique 'Look, cover, write/say, check' to memorise new words.
2 Make links between new words and phrases and a sound, image, word or action that you know.
3 Every time you see an animal, for example, think of any French word you know which would describe it, e.g. colour, size, temperament.
4 List words in a different order – you often remember those at the beginning and end but forget the ones in the middle.
5 Practise vocabulary with a friend.
6 Draw a spider diagram and link words on the same topic.
