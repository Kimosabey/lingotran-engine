# 5C Mardi gras

- ***talk about a fancy dress party***
- ***practise using the verb être (to be)***

[Image: photo of a mardi gras street carnival crowd — masked figures in white masks, dancers in yellow-and-white check costumes, a woman in green top and sunglasses waving]

### Mardi gras

*Shrove Tuesday (mardi gras – literally 'fat' Tuesday) is widely celebrated in France, sometimes with a carnival or fancy dress party. As in the UK, people eat pancakes, a tradition which comes from the old religious practice of eating up all the rich food before fasting in Lent (the 40 days before Easter).*

## 2 C'est qui?

Aujourd'hui, c'est mardi gras. Toutes ces jeunes personnes sont déguisées. Il y a Luc et sa sœur, Coralie. Il y a Sébastien et ses cousins, Olivier et Roseline. Il y a aussi la famille Lambert: Anne-Marie, sa sœur, Suzanne, et ses frères, Christophe et Jean-Pierre.
