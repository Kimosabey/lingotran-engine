**Dossier-langue — Grammaire 11.6**

### Reflexive verbs (*je* form)

| French | English |
| --- | --- |
| *Je me lève* | I get up (or 'I get myself up'). |
| *Je me couche* | I go to bed (or 'I lay myself down') |

Verbs like this are called **reflexive verbs**. They contain an extra word (**me/m'** etc) which is called a reflexive pronoun.

You've also used ***Je m'appelle*** ... (I'm called/I call myself...).

You will learn more about reflexive verbs in Stage 2.
