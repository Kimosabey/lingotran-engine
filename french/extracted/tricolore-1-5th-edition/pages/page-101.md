**Dossier-langue — Grammaire 1.4**

### When to use the definite article (le/la/les)

- ***avoir*** + school subject

With the verb **avoir**, the article (**le**, **la** or **les**) is not used. This is the same as in English.

*Ensuite, on a français.* Next, we have French.

*Cet après-midi, nous avons technologie.*
This afternoon we have IT.

- ***aimer/détester*** + school subject

*J'aime les maths.* I like maths.

*Mes amis et moi détestons le dessin.*
My friends and I hate art.

Is *le/la/les* used with the verbs *aimer* and *détester*?

Say five more sentences to practise this.
