**Stratégies**

### Remembering the gender of nouns (1)

To help you learn the gender of nouns, you could use colour coding. Write or highlight **masculine nouns** in **blue** and **feminine nouns** in **red** – afterwards you will picture each word in red or blue, and that will remind you whether it's feminine or masculine.
