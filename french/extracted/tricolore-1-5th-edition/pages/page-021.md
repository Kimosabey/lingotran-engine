**Stratégies**

**Getting help with words**

Use these phrases to find out the French words for what you want to say or to understand.

*Comment ça se dit?*
How do you say this?

*Comment dit-on […] en français/anglais?*
What's the French/English for […]?

---

ℹ️ 🔊 **L'alphabet**

It can be tricky to remember the way the French say some of the letters of the alphabet, e.g.

e i g j h v w

Think of some ways to jog your memory, e.g.

'i' and 'j' rhyme as in *Fiji*
'g' begins the French word *génie*
'h' sounds like the French word for axe which is *hache*
'w' is pronounced *double v* in French, not 'double u'.
Think of VW cars. The two letters rhyme in French.
