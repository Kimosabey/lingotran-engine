**Stratégies**

### Keeping your conversation going

It is often useful to ask questions to keep a conversation going. Make a note of some useful questions which you could use in different contexts.

*Qu'est-ce que tu fais aujourd'hui/le weekend/ quand il fait froid?*

*Où es-tu? Quel temps fait-il? Tu aimes le sport?*

Sometimes, short questions work well, e.g.

*Et toi?* *Et quand il pleut?*
*Tu aimes ça?* *Avec qui?*
