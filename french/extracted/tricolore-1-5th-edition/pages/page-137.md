### Translating from French to English (5)

Use reading strategies to help you translate into English. Two tips:

- Some verbs and nouns of similar meaning look alike, e.g. *visiter* and *un visiteur*.
- As in English, the prefix *re-* or *ré-* often has the meaning of 'again', e.g. *la réouverture* – the reopening.

Work out the meaning of the nouns.

**1** **entrer** = to go in, enter — *l'entrée (f)* = ?

**2** **sortir** = to go out — *la sortie* = ?

**3** **fermer** = to close — *la fermeture* = ?

**4** **ouvrir** = to open — *l'ouverture* = ?

**5** **nager** = to swim — *un nageur* = ?

Translate these sentences into English.

**1** *Où est l'entrée du parc?*

**2** *Le magasin se trouve près de la sortie.*

**3** *Les dauphins remontent régulièrement à la surface du lac.*
