# 5H Écoutez bien!

- *develop and practise your listening skills*

**[A1 (inferred)]**

## 1 Trouve le bon mot

> First, check that you can recognise words that you have met before.

🔊 Écris 1–6. Écoute et écris la bonne lettre.

**Exemple: 1f**

**a** fille  **b** famille  **c** livre
**d** ville  **e** fils  **f** oui

## 2 C'est quelle image?

> Now listen to some words you might not have met before, then add them to your vocabulary book.
>
> If two nouns sound very similar, listening to the word before the noun might help you to spot which is which, e.g.

[Image: illustration of a yellow house with trees] **une maison**
[Image: illustration of a construction worker in a hard hat holding a hammer and a power drill] **un maçon**

🔊 Écris 1–6. Écoute et écris la bonne lettre.

**Exemple: 1D**

**A** [Image: purple rain cloud with falling rain] **la pluie**
**B** [Image: open green umbrella] **un parapluie**
**C** [Image: glass of orange juice with a straw] **une boisson**
**D** [Image: croissant] **un croissant**
**E** [Image: pharmacy shop front, sign reading "Pharmacie"] **une pharmacie**
**F** [Image: pharmacist in a white coat holding medication] **un pharmacien**

## 3 C'est quel mot?

> Listen really carefully here.

🔊 Écoute. On dit les deux mots, puis on répète un des mots seulement. Écris **a** ou **b**.

**Exemple: 1b**

1 **a** je  **b** j'ai   3 **a** je  **b** j'aime   5 **a** sous  **b** sur
2 **a** j'ai  **b** j'aime   4 **a** trois  **b** toi   6 **a** sœur  **b** sur

## 5 L'histoire de Suzanne

🔊 **a** Écoute l'histoire de Suzanne.

1 Listen to discover what event the speaker is describing.
2 Listen again. This time find out:
   **a** Was the event a complete success?
   **b** Explain why this was.
3 Now fill in more details.
   **c** What date is being described?
   **d** How old is Suzanne?
   **e** Who or what is *citron pressé*?

[Image: two girls — one in a purple top and red skirt handing a gift-wrapped box to another girl wearing a yellow-and-red striped top and dark skirt, who reaches out to take it.]

➕ **b** Écris un résumé de l'histoire de Suzanne. Réécoute l'histoire, si nécessaire.

---

**Stratégies**

### Listening with focus

The first time you listen to an item, aim to get the gist of it – the general idea of what it is about. Then listen again several times and focus on details to build up the meaning gradually.
