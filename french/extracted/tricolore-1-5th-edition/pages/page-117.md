### The verb *prendre* (to take)

**[A1 (inferred)]**

If French-speaking people ask you what you will have to drink or eat, they will probably say:

*Qu'est-ce que tu prends?*

Here is the irregular verb **prendre** in full:

| | Singular | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | je prends | nous prenons |
| 2 | tu prends | vous prenez |
| 3 | il/elle/on prend | ils/elles prennent |
