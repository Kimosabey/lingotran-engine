**Phonétique**

The letter 'h' at the beginning of a word

The letter 'h' at the beginning of a word is usually silent, as if the word begins with a vowel.

*l'homme*

en **h**iver   l'**h**ôpital

Exceptions: le hamster   Le Havre   le hockey

**Say what happens regularly on a particular day**

*Le lundi, le musée est fermé.*
The museum is closed on Mondays.

*Il y a un marché dans les rues le mercredi et le samedi.*
There's a street market on Wednesdays and Saturdays.
