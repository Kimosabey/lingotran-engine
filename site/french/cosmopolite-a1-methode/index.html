Overview

About this book

Organized into 8 dossiers, each opening with discussion prompts and a class project, followed by 6 leçons and a Cultures spread, plus a DELF A1 mock exam and a Précis de grammaire et phonétique reference appendix. Leans heavily on real-world photography and "authentic document" realia (magazine clippings, posters, city maps, screenshots) rather than purely typeset exercises.
