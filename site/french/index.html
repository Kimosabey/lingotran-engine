Overview

Three workbooks, one pipeline

A CEFR A1 coursebook, a verb-conjugation practice book, and a combined vocabulary/grammar revision workbook — each run through the same transcribe → QA → repair cycle.

Level convention differs by book. Cosmopolite 1 is fixed — the whole book is A1, uniformly. The two legacy workbooks don't print a level per exercise; "A1/A2" is the book's overall scope, with a per-exercise level inferred during transcription and always tagged "(inferred)".
