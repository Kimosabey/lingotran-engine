Overview

The French corpus

All three books are scanned images (no text layer) rendered at 300 DPI and recovered through the zero-data-loss pipeline. Cosmopolite 1 is single-page scans; the two legacy workbooks are two-page spreads.

Level convention differs by book: Cosmopolite 1 is fixed — the whole book is A1, uniformly. The two legacy workbooks (Pratique Conjugaison, Pratique Révision) don't print a level per exercise — “A1/A2” is the book's overall CEFR scope, with a per-exercise level inferred during transcription and always tagged “(inferred)”.
